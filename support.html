Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

Go to Settings → Subscriptions → "Restore Purchases". This will restore any active subscriptions you've purchased with your Apple ID.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through the App Store:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap "Subscriptions"
  4. Select Definote Plus
  5. Tap "Cancel Subscription"

You can also manage subscriptions in Settings → Subscriptions → "Manage Subscription".

What's included in the free version?

The free version includes:

  • One free daily challenge every day
  • First 20 levels of each track (40 levels in total, Everyday and SAT/GRE)
  • Basic Word Journal functionality
  • Streak tracking

What does Definote Plus include?

Definote Plus unlocks:

  • All 1,000+ vocabulary levels
  • Unlimited hints (no more getting stuck!)
  • Streak protection (miss a day without losing your streak)

How do I sync my progress across devices?

Definote uses iCloud to sync your progress automatically. Make sure:

  • You're signed into the same iCloud account on all devices
  • iCloud Drive is enabled in Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud
  • Both devices are connected to the internet

Sync may take a few minutes after making changes.

How does the Word Journal work?

Every word you learn in levels is automatically added to your Word Journal. You can review words using spaced repetition to reinforce your learning. Words you mark as "Hard" will appear more frequently in reviews.

How do I change the pronunciation voice?

Go to Settings and toggle between US English and UK English pronunciation. The app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech voices.

Why isn't audio playing?

Definote respects your device's silent switch. If your device is in silent mode, music and sound effects won't play (this is intentional). To hear audio:

  • Turn off silent mode using the switch on the side of your iPhone
  • Or enable music/SFX in Settings even in silent mode

How do Game Center leaderboards work?

Sign into Game Center to compete with friends! Leaderboards track:

  • Longest streak (consecutive days playing)
  • Total words learned

You'll also earn achievements as you progress.
