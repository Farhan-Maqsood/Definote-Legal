Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2024

Introduction

Definote ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection and use when you use our mobile application.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data.

Definote stores all user data locally on your device and optionally syncs it via Apple's iCloud service if you have iCloud enabled in your device settings. We never access, collect, or transmit any of your personal information to our servers or third parties.

Information Stored Locally

The following information is stored only on your device (and optionally in your private iCloud storage):

  • Your vocabulary progress and level completion status
  • Words you've learned and added to your Word Journal
  • Your daily challenge streak and statistics
  • App settings and preferences (audio, voice selection, etc.)
  • Game Center data (leaderboards and achievements)

Apple Services

Definote uses the following Apple services that have their own privacy policies:

  • iCloud: If enabled on your device, your app data syncs across your devices via your private iCloud account. We never access this data.
  • StoreKit: Used for processing in-app subscriptions. Apple handles all payment processing.
  • Game Center: Optional. Used for leaderboards and achievements. Data is managed by Apple.

Third-Party Services

Definote does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no cookies, trackers, or SDKs that collect your information.

Children's Privacy

Definote is designed to be safe for all ages. Since we collect no personal data, there is no risk to children's privacy when using our app.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device
  • You can disable iCloud sync in your device settings
  • You have complete control over your data at all times

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date and posting the new policy in the app and on this page.
